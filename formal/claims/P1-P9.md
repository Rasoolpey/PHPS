## Corrections the proofs ask of the paper

1. Corollary 1: state that each storage is bounded below (the quadratic ones are `≥ 0`; the steam exergy `≥ 0` is
   part of Proposition 1's hypotheses).
2. Backward Euler: "removes stored energy" when the Hessian is positive semidefinite.
3. Mixed midpoint and discrete-gradient rows: one effort vector in every row, output and loss of the step.
4. The power convention of the `dq0` axes: 2/3 of the phase power.
5. The composition gives the balance of the whole; the assembled realization after eliminating the algebraic voltages is `kron_dissipative` (needs `(J - R)_WW` invertible, which the paper should state: every algebraic node has a conductance).

## What this does not prove

- Proposition 1 for each device (P10-P13) and Proposition 2 (P14): the device matrices are not yet written.
- The steam unit's first-law ledger as a separate discrete identity (review 123, item 7).
- That the Julia code computes these equations (numerical structure check, out of scope here).
- Existence or uniqueness of the implicit step; accuracy; anything about the simulated numbers.
